# Page Quality Levels: Low, Medium, High, Highest (QRG 3.1-3.2, 5-9)

Contents: 1. The 9 considerations · 2. MC quality dimensions · 3. Low · 4. Medium · 5. High · 6. Highest · 7. Special page types · 8. Rating tie-breaks

## 1. What a PQ rating considers (3.1)

Purpose · topic and YMYL level · potential for harm · type of website (hobby vs corporate, transactional vs not, volunteer vs professional) · info the site/creator gives about itself · MC quality · title · role of Ads and SC · reputation of site and creator · trustworthiness (E-E-A-T). They overlap - inaccuracies found while reading MC lower Trust; expertise found in reputation research raises it.

## 2. MC quality dimensions (3.2)

- **Effort**: how much a human actively worked to make it satisfying. Effort can also be in building tools/functionality. Auto-generating thousands of pages through translation without oversight = no effort. On forums, participation and depth count as effort.
- **Originality**: unique content not available elsewhere; is this the original source?
- **Talent or skill**: enough to give a satisfying experience.
- **Accuracy**: informational pages must be accurate; YMYL must match well-established expert consensus.

What counts depends on purpose: consensus for medical advice, skill for how-to, talent/originality for art. Test the page like a real user: read it, use the calculator, add a product to cart, play the game.

Title is part of MC: a good title summarizes the MC so users can decide whether to click.

## 3. Low (Section 5) - any one is enough

| Consideration | Low trigger |
|---|---|
| MC | Not enough effort, originality, talent or skill for the purpose; mild inaccuracies |
| Title | Slightly misleading, shocking or exaggerated |
| Ads / SC | Significantly distract from or interrupt MC |
| Site / creator info | Unsatisfying amount for the purpose (alias is fine for personal UGC) |
| Reputation | Mildly negative |
| E-E-A-T | Inadequate for the purpose |

Key notes:
- **Lacking E-E-A-T (5.1)**: restaurant review by someone who never ate there; skydiving guide by a non-expert; tax forms on a cooking site; shop with minimal customer service info. Nothing - not even good reputation - compensates for lacking E-E-A-T for the topic.
- **Low quality MC examples (5.2)**: craft tutorial with filler at the top; "best vacuums" list paraphrased from other reviews (no first-hand experience); top-10 slideshow of others' photos and lists; "how many cm in a meter" buried in off-topic content; "tallest mountains" list missing Everest.
- **Low quality titles**: "Part 2 coming soon!" with only rumours; sensational headline over a mundane story; "Eat the healthiest foods in the WORLD!!!!" for basic tips.
- **Low effort / low originality / low added value (5.2.1)**: reposts with little comment; pages of embedded videos or repinned images with little curation; "best" lists built from existing reviews.
- **Filler (5.2.2)**: put the most helpful MC at the top (e.g. the recipe itself near the top of a recipe page). Supporting content is fine lower down. Low if there is a lot of unhelpful filler, or prominent filler that makes the useful MC hard to find.
- **Distracting Ads/SC (5.3)**: hard-to-close ads that follow scrolling, interstitials demanding app download, sexual or grotesque ad images, shocking ad headlines. One pop-up with a clear close button is acceptable.
- **Mildly negative reputation (5.4)**: read negative reviews in detail - a few bad customer-service reviews are normal.
- **Unsatisfying site/creator info (5.5)**: stores and payment pages need solid customer service info (email + address alone may be insufficient); YMYL pages need clear responsibility.
- **Exaggerated self-claims (5.6)**: overstated "I'm an expert" claims → Low; base E-E-A-T on MC, reputation and verifiable credentials.

## 4. Medium (Section 6)

Beneficial purpose, achieves it, no harm, title summarizes the page, ads don't interfere, adequate site info, adequate E-E-A-T, reputation neutral.
Two kinds:
- **Nothing wrong, nothing special** - very common
- **Mixed** - some High signals plus one mild Low signal

Typical/average content on a topic is Medium.

## 5. High (Section 7) - no Low problems, plus at least one of:

- MC with high effort, originality, talent or skill that achieves the purpose well
- Positive reputation of the site (if it made the MC) or of the creator, for this topic
- High E-E-A-T for the purpose

High-MC contrasts (7.1):

| High | Low |
|---|---|
| Well-organized, edited, curated; accurate news with depth; forum with meaningful multi-person discussion; clear tutorial | Helpful content mixed with filler; superficial forum replies; tutorial with filler on top |
| Original photos/video; unique first-hand perspective | Info summarized from others; borrowed photos; summary of others' reviews |
| Skill shows in the MC (plumbing guide by a plumber) | Lack of skill prevents purpose |

Reputation for non-YMYL can be informal (popularity, engagement, reviews). No reputation info doesn't block High.
E-E-A-T (7.3): experience matters for almost every topic; expertise needed from photography to taxes; authority (government for tax forms, local business for local info); trust essential for transactions and YMYL, and needed to some degree for reviews and advice.

## 6. Highest (Section 8) - at least one of:

- Very high quality MC: among the most outstanding on the topic online
  - News: original reporting that reveals new information, describes primary sources, meets journalistic standards
  - Art: unique work by highly skilled creators
  - Information: original, accurate, comprehensive, clearly communicated, reflects expert consensus
- Very positive reputation (YMYL: endorsed by known experts/professional bodies, prestigious awards)
- Very high E-E-A-T: uniquely authoritative go-to source; wealth of experience; very high expertise

## 7. Special page types (Section 9)

- **Encyclopedia/wiki**: High only with good reputation and well-referenced articles; check each article; inaccuracy → Low/Lowest.
- **Error/404 pages**: custom 404 that explains and helps navigate = usually Medium; very helpful, original 404 = High/Highest. Temporarily broken page = Low; same problem across many pages (systemic) = Lowest. Pages with no MC across the site, just ads = Lowest.
- **Forums/Q&A**: MC = question + answers + discussion; rate as a visitor; usernames fine; judge E-E-A-T from the posts. New unanswered question = Medium. Long-unanswered or superficial = can be Low. Harmful advice = Lowest. Messy but insightful = Medium. Ad formatted like an answer = misleading design.
- **Non-HTML (PDF, images)**: apply judgment; some criteria won't apply.

## 8. Tie-breaks (10.0)

- Torn between two ratings → choose the lower. Between three → the middle.
- Don't lower PQ because the site is from another country.
- If a page is truly High or Low for a reason not covered, rate it so and explain.
- "Does it look good?" is not the test.
