# Scope Reference: Common Organizations in UK Health & Environmental Content

This file is a quick-reference to prevent attribution scope mismatch - the most common and hardest-to-detect reference error in AI-assisted content.

---

## Air Quality & Environment

### EEA - European Environment Agency
- **Measures:** Ambient (outdoor) air pollution across EU + EEA member countries
- **Does NOT measure:** Indoor air quality, occupational exposure
- **Key outputs:** Annual air quality in Europe report; burden of disease briefings
- **Geographic scope:** EU-27 + EEA cooperating countries (not UK post-Brexit, though UK data may appear in historical reports)
- **Current domain:** eea.europa.eu
- **Correct attribution example:** "239,000 deaths in the EU in 2022 were attributable to PM2.5 exposure (EEA, 2024)" ✅
- **Wrong attribution example:** "154,000 indoor air pollution deaths (EEA)" ❌ EEA does not publish indoor figures

### DEFRA - Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs
- **Measures:** UK outdoor air quality monitoring (AURN network); emissions inventories
- **Does NOT measure:** Indoor air quality; health outcomes
- **Geographic scope:** England primarily; some UK-wide
- **Current domain:** gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-environment-food-rural-affairs

### UKHSA - UK Health Security Agency (formerly Public Health England until 2021)
- **Measures:** UK public health; air quality health impacts; environmental hazards; infectious disease
- **Key outputs:** Indoor air quality reviews (e.g. Cheek et al. 2021 portable air purifier review)
- **Geographic scope:** UK
- **Current domain:** ukhsa.gov.uk
- **Note:** Pre-2021 publications may be attributed to "Public Health England (PHE)" - both are valid if the publication date matches

### WHO - World Health Organization
- **Measures:** Global and regional health statistics; indoor AND outdoor air quality
- **Key outputs:** WHO Air Quality Guidelines (2021); Global Burden of Disease
- **Geographic scope:** Global; WHO European Region covers 53 countries (wider than EU)
- **Important:** "WHO European Region" ≠ "EU". WHO European Region includes Russia, Central Asia, Turkey. Always specify if citing WHO European Region vs EU.
- **Current domain:** who.int

### Met Office
- **Measures:** UK weather; pollen forecasts and calendar; UV index
- **Does NOT measure:** Air pollution levels (that is DEFRA/UKHSA)
- **Current domain:** metoffice.gov.uk

## UK Health & Respiratory

### Asthma + Lung UK (formerly British Lung Foundation)
- **Name change:** British Lung Foundation merged with Asthma UK in 2020; rebranded as Asthma + Lung UK
- **Old domain:** blf.org.uk (may still partially redirect but do not use for new citations)
- **Current domain:** asthmaandlung.org.uk
- **Measures:** UK respiratory disease prevalence; patient surveys; lung health policy
- **Key stat:** "12.7 million people in the UK have a history of asthma, COPD or another longstanding respiratory illness" - this comes from the BLF Respiratory Health of the Nation project (still valid data, cite as Asthma + Lung UK formerly British Lung Foundation)
- **Does NOT measure:** Air quality data; environmental pollution

### NHS
- **Measures:** Clinical guidance (via NICE); England hospital data; patient health information
- **Geographic scope:** England primarily (NHS Scotland, NHS Wales, HSC Northern Ireland are separate)
- **Current domain:** nhs.uk
- **Does NOT measure:** Environmental pollution; EU/global statistics

### NICE - National Institute for Health and Care Excellence
- **Measures:** Clinical guidelines; technology appraisals; evidence reviews
- **Geographic scope:** England (with some UK-wide application)
- **Current domain:** nice.org.uk

### Allergy UK (formerly British Allergy Foundation / BAF)
- **Current name:** Allergy UK
- **Old name:** British Allergy Foundation (BAF) - the "BAF" acronym still appears in some Coway product certifications
- **Current domain:** allergyuk.org
- **What the certification covers:** Tests products and awards Seal of Approval to those that capture airborne allergens at defined levels. Not a medical certification.

## Academic Journals Commonly Cited in Air Quality Content

| Abbreviated name | Full name | Publisher | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEA | Clinical & Experimental Allergy | Wiley | Allergy and immunology research |
| Indoor Air | Indoor Air | Wiley | Indoor environment and health |
| STE | Science of the Total Environment | Elsevier | Environmental science |
| CID | Clinical Infectious Diseases | Oxford/IDSA | Infectious disease |
| Allergy | Allergy | Wiley | Allergy and immunology |

**When verifying journal citations, cross-check:**
1. Volume and issue number against PubMed or publisher page
2. Year of publication matches the volume year
3. Author list - AI sometimes transposes or misremembers first authors

## Common Scope Errors to Watch For

| Claim as written | What's wrong | Correct framing |
|---|---|---|
| "EEA: 154,000 deaths from indoor air pollution" | EEA covers outdoor air only | Use WHO if indoor figure needed; or use EEA's outdoor PM2.5 figure |
| "NHS data shows indoor pollution 2-5x outdoor" | NHS does not publish this data | EPA (cited by UKHSA/WHO) is the source of this figure |
| "British Lung Foundation 2024 statistics" | BLF no longer exists | Asthma + Lung UK (formerly British Lung Foundation) |
| "UK deaths from air pollution per WHO European Region data" | WHO European Region ≠ UK | Specify UK-specific data or note this is a wider regional figure |
| "DEFRA confirms indoor air quality risks" | DEFRA measures outdoor air | UKHSA or WHO for indoor air quality |
