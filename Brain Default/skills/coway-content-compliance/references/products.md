Every speed and coverage claim needs its test basis stated. The Airmega 50 line does this correctly ("based on a 2.4 m ceiling height") and is the pattern to follow; the others should carry the equivalent qualifier once confirmed.

Filtration, all models: HyperCaptive Filtration System removes 99.999% of airborne particles down to 0.01 µm. Typical stack is a vacuumable or washable pre-filter, activated carbon or deodorisation filter, and a HEPA filter. Attribute the 99.999% / 0.01 µm figure to **HyperCaptive Filtration**, never to "HEPA".

Model-specific features worth using because they are checkable: Airmega 50 night light and compact footprint for a bedside table; Airmega 100 UK 3-pin plug, colour-coded LED ring, no app required; Airmega 150 top-loading pre-filter that pulls out from the top, Quiet Mark certification; Mighty optional ioniser with on/off control, Smart Eco Mode; JET six airflow levels with Focus and Wide modes; 350 HyperVortex 360° intake, antistatic surface, rear carry handle, roughly 58 cm tall and 7.8 kg, day/night light-adaptive behaviour, timer, child lock; 450 laser sensor detecting PM1, PM2.5 and PM10 in real time, full-spectrum AQI display, Eco Mode pausing after 10 minutes of clean air and rechecking after 30.

Balance is allowed and improves credibility: independent reviews note the Airmega 350's sensor detects PM10 only, not PM2.5 or PM1, and reacts slowly to sudden changes. Stating a genuine limitation is safer than implying capability the sensor does not have.

**Resolved and approved:**
- **Airmega 350 coverage is 185 m².** "~148 m²" in older Amazon copy is wrong as a coverage figure: 148 m² is the speed claim (148 m² in 30 minutes), not the rated room size. Correct any asset that gives 148 m² as coverage (MAJOR, fact).
- **Airmega Mighty has been NYT Wirecutter's #1 Pick for 12 years in total.** Use "NYT Wirecutter #1 Pick for 12 years" in every asset and locale. "10 years", "11 years" or any other duration is outdated and must be corrected (CRITICAL, fact).

Over 30 years of innovation (In 1989, Coway began water purifier business). Trusted in more than 15 million homes worldwide. Korean engineering, with Coway designing, developing, manufacturing and testing in-house.

Note the wording drifts across existing assets between "15 million homes", "15 million customers" and "15 million air purifiers sold" - these are three different claims. Use one approved form consistently and flag the inconsistency.

Avoid "healthier air to 15 million homes" - the number is fine, the health framing is not.

## Known conflicts to resolve

Do not resolve these silently. Flag them in the report and ask the Coway team which figure is approved.

1. **Airmega 50 CADR** - 120 m³/h versus 120.1 m³/h across assets. Minor, but pick one.
2. **Brand reach wording** - homes vs customers vs units sold.
3. **Speed-claim test conditions** - only the Airmega 50 states a ceiling height. The others need their basis confirmed before the speed claim is repeated.
4. **Airmega 350 filter price** - roughly €79.99 on Coway.de; prices change and are market-specific, so verify before publishing rather than carrying the figure across.
