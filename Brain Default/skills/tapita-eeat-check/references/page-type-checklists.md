# Checklists by Page Type

Each checklist applies QRG principles to a common SEO page type. Section numbers in brackets point to the QRG source. Use the relevant list in addition to the Lowest/Low screening.

Contents: 1. Product page · 2. Category/collection page · 3. Blog / how-to / informational · 4. Review & "best X" affiliate content · 5. YMYL health/wellness · 6. YMYL finance/legal · 7. Local business / service page · 8. News · 9. Forum / UGC / Q&A · 10. Homepage, About, Contact · 11. 404 & error pages · 12. AI-assisted or programmatic content at scale

## 1. Product page (2.4.1, 2.5.3, 3.4, 5.5, 21.0)
- [ ] MC includes title, description, specs, images, price, availability; tabs (reviews, shipping, safety info) count as MC - make them useful
- [ ] Original product photos/video where possible; not only manufacturer copy duplicated across many retailers (low originality)
- [ ] Add-to-cart and checkout actually work (raters test the cart)
- [ ] Customer service: contact, returns, exchanges, payment, shipping, warranty - easy to find
- [ ] Secure payment; company identity clear
- [ ] Product claims accurate; health/safety claims substantiated (YMYL if the product can cause harm)
- [ ] Reviews genuine; no fake testimonials
- [ ] Title/H1 describes the product accurately; no exaggeration

## 2. Category / collection page (17.0, 21.0, 5.2.2)
- [ ] Product grid is the MC and appears first; intro text short and genuinely useful (buying guidance, not keyword filler)
- [ ] Supports browsing and research: filters, sorting, comparison, specs, prices
- [ ] Popular/prominent options represented for broad queries
- [ ] Any long-form copy placed below products, written with expertise (e.g. how to choose), not generic
- [ ] Links to sub-collections and guides help users (SC)

## 3. Blog / how-to / informational article (3.2, 5.2, 5.2.2, 7.1)
- [ ] Answer or instructions near the top; no long generic intro, commonly-known filler, or off-topic padding
- [ ] Clear structure, edited, accurate; steps actually work (written by someone with the skill)
- [ ] Original value: first-hand experience, own photos, data, examples, unique insight vs top results
- [ ] Named author with relevant expertise/experience; dates published/updated
- [ ] Sources cited for facts; consistent with consensus
- [ ] Title summarizes the content; not clickbait
- [ ] Not a paraphrase/summary of existing articles (check with sentence searches)

## 4. Reviews and "best X" lists (3.4, 4.5.3, 5.1, 5.2, 5.2.1)
- [ ] Evidence the reviewer used the product: own photos, test methods, measurements, durations, pros/cons from use
- [ ] No false claims of testing (deceptive → Lowest)
- [ ] Not built from summarizing other people's reviews (Low)
- [ ] Written to help buyers decide, not solely to push affiliate clicks; balanced drawbacks
- [ ] Affiliate relationships disclosed; affiliate links handled appropriately
- [ ] Manufacturer self-reviews and paid promotions recognised as conflicts of interest
- [ ] Clear reviewer identity and why they're qualified

## 5. YMYL health / wellness (2.3, 3.4.1, 4.4, 5.1)
- [ ] Every health claim consistent with expert consensus; cite health authorities or peer-reviewed sources
- [ ] No cure/prevention claims that contradict consensus (risk of Lowest)
- [ ] Author or reviewer with verifiable medical/scientific credentials for advice and facts; personal stories framed as experience
- [ ] Clear site ownership, contact, editorial/medical review policy
- [ ] Safe guidance: when to see a doctor, risks, contraindications
- [ ] Title not exaggerated or fear-based
- [ ] Credentials not overstated or invented

## 6. YMYL finance / legal / government info (2.3, 3.4.1, 4.4)
- [ ] Facts current (rates, laws, deadlines, forms); dates shown
- [ ] Advice from qualified professionals; disclaimers where appropriate
- [ ] No guaranteed-return or misleading claims
- [ ] Links to official sources (regulators, government portals)
- [ ] Transparent ownership and contact; licensing info where required locally

## 7. Local business / service page (2.5.3, 12.7.4, 22.0)
- [ ] Real address, phone, hours, map; consistent with business profiles
- [ ] No fake physical location or fake storefront photos (deceptive → Lowest)
- [ ] Service area and local relevance clear
- [ ] Reputation: reviews on independent platforms; respond to complaints
- [ ] Doorway-style city pages with swapped place names = scaled/low value

## 8. News (8.1, 18.0, 4.4)
- [ ] Accurate, dated, attributed; primary sources described
- [ ] Original reporting where possible (Highest signal)
- [ ] Headline matches the story
- [ ] Clear newsroom ownership, editorial standards, corrections policy
- [ ] Syndicated content properly licensed/labelled

## 9. Forum / UGC / Q&A (9.3, 4.6.2)
- [ ] Moderation removes spam/bot link comments (many spam comments → Lowest for that page)
- [ ] Ads not formatted like answers
- [ ] Encourage depth and respectful discussion; expert or experienced members highlighted
- [ ] Harmful advice moderated, especially on YMYL threads
- [ ] Thin/unanswered old threads consolidated or noindexed

## 10. Homepage, About, Contact (2.5)
- [ ] Logo links home; navigation clear
- [ ] About explains who runs the site, mission, team, history; no exaggeration
- [ ] Contact proportionate to purpose; customer-service hub for shops
- [ ] Author pages with bios, credentials and external profiles
- [ ] Policies: privacy, terms, returns, editorial/AI-use policy where relevant

## 11. 404 & error pages (9.2)
- [ ] Custom 404 explaining the problem, with search and helpful links (Medium → High if genuinely helpful)
- [ ] No soft-404 pages with ads only
- [ ] Check for systemic broken templates across the site (systemic → Lowest)

## 12. AI-assisted or programmatic content at scale (4.6.5, 4.6.6, 4.5.3)
- [ ] Each page offers unique value vs the web (data, experience, tools, expert review) - not template + swapped keywords
- [ ] Human editing and fact-checking; no leftover AI phrases
- [ ] No invented author personas or AI headshots
- [ ] No scraping/auto-translating/synonymizing others' content
- [ ] Not a network of sites hiding scale
- [ ] Sample 5-10 pages: if they read interchangeably, flag scaled content abuse risk
- [ ] Third-party/partner sections on-topic for the host site (avoid site reputation abuse)
- [ ] Newly acquired domains: content consistent with domain's history (avoid expired domain abuse)
