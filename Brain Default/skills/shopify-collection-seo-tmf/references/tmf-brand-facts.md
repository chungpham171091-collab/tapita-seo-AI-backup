## Unresolved - do not write until TMF decides

### Company history

Three different answers currently exist across one content set:

- Six audited pages say "since 2008"
- One says "over a decade"
- TMF's own About Us page says the business "has been in business for 41+ years"

Nobody has confirmed which is correct, and the forum's founding date and the company's founding date are probably different things.

**Until TMF confirms:** write the Why Buy section without a founding date. "TMF Store is built for working carpet cleaning professionals" carries the same trust signal without the risk. When TMF does decide, the copy should also say *what* the date refers to - the forum or the company.

### Community size

- "over 50,000 professionals on the TMF forum" appeared on one audited page and is unverifiable. The forum publishes no member count.
- "thousands of professionals" appeared on another and is the defensible formulation.

**Use "thousands of professionals",** or get a real number from the forum admin and cite it.

### Free shipping threshold

A free-shipping condition on chemical orders over $300 appears on at least one product listing. It has not been verified as a site-wide policy.

**Until verified:** do not state it as a site-wide offer. Either verify the policy page or attribute it to the specific product where it appears.

## Wording that works

The strongest verified trust signals for a Why Buy section, in rough order of usefulness:

1. Products selected and tested against real commercial use conditions rather than consumer benchmarks.
2. Direct technical support from people who use the equipment - with the phone number and hours.
3. Honest review data from working technicians, which TMF genuinely has.
4. Military and first responder pricing across chemicals and equipment.
5. TMF Rewards.
6. Financing on equipment purchases.
7. The forum community, described as "thousands of professionals" or by the forum's own "world's largest" wording.

Keep the section 60-100 words. Pick three or four of these, not all seven.

**Avoid:** competitor-bashing that makes no claim about TMF ("field-tested by operators who clean 8 to 10 hours a day, not in a controlled lab by technicians running a single demo weekly"). One audited page spent 400+ words on this with no product links and no sourced claims. It ranks for nothing and dilutes the credible material around it.
